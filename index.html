01 / The opportunity

Different collections. One unmistakable brand.

Rimoka already offers a broad range of nail styles, from everyday neutrals and French tips to floral, chrome and seasonal designs. The opportunity is to make each collection feel like its own world while keeping the product itself unmistakably accurate.
01

Give collections a mood

Build a recognisable visual story around seasonal colour, nail art and the feeling behind each launch.

02

Protect the product

Keep the real nail shape, length, finish, pattern and packaging faithful to Rimoka’s supplied photography.

03

Make each idea travel

Plan campaign concepts that can flex across product pages, feed posts, stories, launch assets and ads.

02 / Proposed creative routes

Four ways to tell the product story.

Choose a route to see how one visual idea could serve a different customer moment. These are concept directions created for this proposal, not existing Rimoka campaigns.

01 · Editorial beauty

The close-up story

Skin, texture and controlled light bring attention to the shape, finish and detail of the manicure.

02 · Product-led campaign

Details worth noticing

A considered product still life that lets colour, finish and nail art do the selling without visual clutter.

03 · Everyday lifestyle

Made to be worn

Natural-feeling moments that help customers picture the manicure in their own routines and occasions.

04 · Social-first variation

One idea, more moments

Crop and reframe the approved campaign into short, clear visual stories for launches and social publishing.

All visuals in this presentation are original illustrative AI concepts. They are not Rimoka product photography. Final campaign work would use Rimoka’s actual product images as the source of truth.

03 / How I would work

AI creates possibilities. Art direction makes them usable.

The value is not simply generating an attractive image. It is turning a creative idea into a polished commercial asset without letting the generated scene change the product customers are actually buying.

01

Start with the real product

Review the supplied nail set, packaging, references, collection story and where the visual will appear.

02

Art-direct the scene

Choose the composition, model styling, surfaces, lighting and colour palette to complement that specific set.

03

Generate, composite and refine

Build the environment with AI, then use the real product photography for accurate nail details. Retouch and colour-correct the final image.

04

Prepare the final formats

Deliver high-resolution assets and platform-specific crops, with editable files where agreed.

The quality check

Keep the details true.

Before delivery, I would check every image against the original product reference.

Nail shape and length
Exact colour and finish
Patterns and nail art
Packaging and logo accuracy

02 / Social

03 / Campaigns

04 / Paid media

Ad variations

Alternative hooks and compositions to test while retaining product accuracy.

05 / A practical first 30 days

Start with one collection. Build a repeatable rhythm.

I would begin with one agreed product or collection, prove the visual direction, then extend the approved concept into the formats Rimoka needs. The scope and asset volume can be agreed around your launch calendar.
