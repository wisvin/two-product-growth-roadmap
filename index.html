01 / The opportunity

Make the impact impossible to miss.

Happy Paws already has the most important ingredient: genuine rescue stories. The opportunity is to build a consistent content system around those stories, reach people beyond Stockton and make it easier for supporters to take the next step.

01 — DISCOVERY

Reach people who haven't met Happy Paws yet.

Use short-form video, shareable education and relevant partnerships to introduce the rescue to more UK cat lovers and potential supporters.

Reach & visibility
02 — CONNECTION

Give every rescue journey a voice.

Show progress, care, foster moments and successful adoptions. Build emotional connection without relying only on distress or urgent appeals.

Trust & engagement
03 — ACTION

Make helping feel simple.

Give each campaign one clear next step: adopt, donate, volunteer, foster, share or become a regular supporter.

Support & enquiries
✦

Start with the journey, not just the post. The public website already highlights adoption, donations, volunteering and newsletter sign-up. The first audit will check how clear and mobile-friendly those routes are, then connect social content to them consistently.

02 / First 30 days

A clear sequence. Less founder follow-up.

The first month moves from understanding the current position to creating, publishing and improving. The workflow is designed so the rescue team can share source material and essential updates without having to direct every marketing task.

Week 01 · Foundation
01

Audit & direction

Understand what exists and where the biggest opportunities are.

  • Review social channels and website journeys
  • Set baseline performance measures
  • Research comparable UK rescue brands
  • Agree voice, access and approval flow
Week 02 · Production
02

Build the story system

Turn available rescue material into repeatable formats.

  • Define content pillars
  • Build a 30-day calendar
  • Create reusable creative templates
  • Prepare the first campaign concept
Week 03 · Activation
03

Publish & test

Launch consistently and learn what earns attention.

  • Publish platform-native content
  • Test hooks and calls to action
  • Engage with relevant communities
  • Identify collaboration prospects
Week 04 · Optimisation
04

Measure & refine

Keep what works and improve the next month.

  • Review reach, saves, shares and clicks
  • Review donation and enquiry routes
  • Summarise learning and opportunities
  • Plan the next 60 days
03 / Content direction

Four pillars. One recognisable voice.

The mix should make people feel, learn, participate and act. Rescue stories lead, but the feed should also celebrate progress, useful knowledge and the people who make the work possible.

♡

Rescue to home

Before-and-after stories, recovery updates, foster diaries, adoption profiles and “where are they now?” follow-ups.

✳

Care & education

Cat welfare tips, myth-busting, responsible ownership and clear explanations of the care cats receive.

☻

The people behind the paws

Volunteer spotlights, foster thank-yous, supporter stories and behind-the-scenes moments.

♥

Ways to help

Donation impact, regular giving, wish-list needs, volunteering, fostering and adoption calls to action.
