01 — The opportunity

Two products.
Two different reasons to care.

Each product line deserves its own audience insight, positioning and route to conversion. The first task is to understand what makes each one relevant to the people most likely to buy.

The actual product identities and analytics will be reviewed after the confidentiality agreement and access are provided.
Product line / A

Find the reason to choose.

Clarify the target segment, customer motivation, competitive alternatives and strongest product message. Build a marketing direction around those findings.

Product line / B

Create a separate path to discovery.

Develop its own buyer persona, positioning, content themes and landing-page journey. Avoid copying the first product's strategy where the audiences differ.

01

Understand the customer

Research each product, define customer segments and buyer personas, compare competitors and establish distinct brand positioning.

02

Build the marketing direction

Prioritize channels, especially Instagram and Facebook. Create content pillars, campaign ideas and a separate 30-day calendar for each product line.

03

Improve the website journey

Refresh the product pages, create or improve two landing pages, strengthen product copy and imagery, clarify calls to action and test desktop and mobile.

04

Support search and AI discovery

Apply on-page SEO fundamentals. For GEO and AEO, make product facts clear, answer common customer questions and organize useful information so search and AI answer systems can interpret it.

05

Measure and prioritize

Recommend KPIs, tracking methods, practical budgets and a prioritized 90-day roadmap. Establish the baseline before setting performance targets.

04 — Search visibility

Show up where
customers are searching.

SEO, GEO and AEO overlap, but each highlights a different discovery experience. The practical starting point is clear, accurate and helpful product information.

01 / SEO

Be relevant in search.

Improve page titles, headings, descriptions, internal links, image alt text and product-focused keyword relevance.

02 / GEO

Be understandable to AI.

Use consistent product and brand facts, clear entities, credible context and well-organized information that generative systems can interpret.

03 / AEO

Answer the real question.

Structure direct answers to common customer questions, useful FAQs and concise sections that match search intent.
